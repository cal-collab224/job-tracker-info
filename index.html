Private personal application

A clearer view of a job search.

Job Tracker 2027 helps its owner monitor vacancies, review opportunities and prepare a weekly email digest. The owner's connected Excel workbook remains the source of truth.

These public pages explain the application. They do not display vacancies, application records, account connections or the private dashboard.

01

Check configured sources

The tracker checks enabled employer sources and uses configured search services and supported feeds to discover opportunities beyond that list. It reports unsuccessful checks.

02

Review in Excel

Discovered vacancies are checked against official or employer-authorised pages where possible. The owner keeps or dismisses discoveries and manages application statuses and notes in Excel.

03

Receive a digest

When configured and authorised, Gmail sends the complete weekly report to the owner's single configured reporting destination. It includes opportunities, changes, deadlines and source failures.

Account access stays with the owner

The tracker is intended for the owner's personal use and requires sign-in. Microsoft authorisation connects the owner's workbook. Google authorisation requests only Gmail's send permission; the application does not request access to read the inbox.

For support, use the user support email displayed on the application's Google authorisation screen. Read the privacy policy for the data used by the tracker and ways to revoke access.
