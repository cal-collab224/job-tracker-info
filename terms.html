Personal use

Job Tracker 2027 is intended for its owner's personal vacancy search. Its private dashboard and account connections are not a public service or an invitation for other people to register. Use connected accounts and source access only where authorised.

Review before applying

The tracker helps organise and assess advertised opportunities. Vacancy information can change, be incomplete or be unavailable. Check the official employer or authorised recruitment page before relying on a deadline, salary, eligibility requirement, location or start date.

A recommendation does not guarantee eligibility, an interview or employment. An unsuccessful check does not establish that a vacancy has closed. The owner makes all application decisions and submissions.

Availability and connected services

Checks and email depend on hosting services, source availability, account permissions and provider quotas. Scheduled execution and delivery times are not guaranteed. The owner should inspect run history and reported failures and keep independent copies of information they need.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains account access and data handling. For questions, contact the owner using the user support email shown on the application's Google authorisation screen.
